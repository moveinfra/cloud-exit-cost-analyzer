# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place to ask questions and contribute, whatever your background or experience.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and accept that
people may disagree with you.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualised language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

**Reporting:** email **hello@moveinfra.eu**. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments,
close threads, or block people who break these rules, and will explain their decision to the person involved.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
